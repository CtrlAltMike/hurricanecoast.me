# Targeted guide review — November 16, 2026

## Scope and outcome

Read the first-aid, car, baby/child, senior/medical, documents and pet kit prose, and sampled the wider production guide inventory. This was **not** a completed full-site factual/medical review. The October 9 full-page review dates remain unchanged. Eight changed HTML pages now carry November 16 modification dates; the six web guides have explicit targeted-update notes, and the two infant printables identify their feeding update.

Changes mirrored in English and Spanish:
- First-aid guide: remove antihistamines as generic sleep support; direct medication selection to pharmacist/label and explicitly exclude using diphenhydramine to make children sleepy. Remove the claim that a thermometer differentiates heat exhaustion from other illness.
- Car guide: specify that N95s filter particles, not gases/vapors, and do not justify entering chemical-hazard areas.
- Baby/child guide and printable: prefer ready-to-feed formula in single-serving containers. Explain that powdered formula is not sterile and needs safe preparation and cleaning; remove unsupported assertions about which formula sells out first or is available at aid points.
